The library, locked

The filter is a value beside the title — tap it and the five choices open in place — so the list starts as high as it can, and progress is a band inside each cover's own bottom edge, lifted off the artwork by a hairline.

Everything works: the filter filters, search filters as you type, a row opens the player, and the ebook-only title opens the reader, which is under construction and says so. The + on the right rail imports from Files: pick an audiobook or an ebook, the file is read, and the metadata it carries is what you confirm before it joins the list. Turn the guides on in the bar below: one inset, one cover column, one tab-bar line.
