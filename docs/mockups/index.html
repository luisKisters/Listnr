One book, three formats, one position

V0 is the audiobook player and note capture, and nothing else. The four tabs — Library · Audiobook · Reader · Scan — are all present from day one because the shell is the thing being confirmed; the two that are not built say so instead of showing dead controls.

Library B is locked: the filter is a value beside the title and progress is a band inside each cover's bottom edge. Player A is locked: artwork on the rails, title, author and chapter as one text group, then scrubber, transport and two quiet utilities — speed and the note key low, the sleep timer up top. Everything on every phone is live. Two colour schemes and an alignment-guide overlay sit in the bar at the bottom of every page and follow you between them.

Plan

  1. 01

    Player V0

    M4B chapters, background audio, lock-screen controls, speed, sleep timer.

    Import via Files is V0 too: M4B and MP3 for the audiobook, EPUB for the ebook.

    AVFoundation · MPNowPlayingInfoCenter · SwiftData

  2. 02

    Notes

    Part of V0: the pencil on the player captures a timestamped note, and the audio pauses while you write and resumes when you are done.

    SwiftData · App Intents

  3. 03

    Scan-sync

    Camera reads a paper page, matches the paired EPUB, moves both positions.

    Vision OCR · ZIPFoundation · SwiftSoup · own fuzzy match

  4. 04

    TestFlight

    Daily use on one device, then a small invite build.

    Xcode Cloud · TestFlight
